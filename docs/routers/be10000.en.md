# VPN on Xiaomi BE10000 and BE10000 PRO

The **Xiaomi Router BE10000** and **BE10000 PRO** are the top models of the Wi-Fi 7 line. Enodia installs on them on
top of the stock firmware: the wizard opens SSH by itself and installs the web panel, the VPN is set up in the browser.

## What works

- **AmneziaWG**, **Xray (VLESS, Reality)**, **Hysteria2** — through your own server, a subscription or an Amnezia
  Premium key.
- **WARP** and **ByeDPI** — without a server of your own. **Zapret** has not been validated on the BE10000.
- Up to six additional exits, a watchdog with backup servers, encrypted DNS, ad blocking, access schedules, home
  access, traffic by device.

## About this model

- **ARM64 architecture** — the same protocol programs as on the BE7000.
- **The flash is split into several volumes**, and Enodia's files are not in the one you see first. The panel and
  «Components» count free space exactly where the protocols live, so «not enough space» shows up only when there
  really is not enough.

## How to install

Download the wizard from the [Releases](https://github.com/Axel173/enodia/releases) page, run it on a computer on the
router's network and follow the steps. Details — [Installation](../install.md).
