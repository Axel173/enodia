# AmneziaWG, Xray and Zapret on Xiaomi BE7000

The **Xiaomi Router BE7000** is the project's main model: Enodia started on it, and every version is checked on it.
Everything installs on top of the stock firmware, without OpenWrt.

## What works on the BE7000

- **AmneziaWG**, **Xray (VLESS, Reality)**, **Hysteria2** — through your own server, a subscription (3x-ui included)
  or an Amnezia Premium key.
- **WARP** — the router gets it by itself; as a separate exit or as a «road» to a server whose address your ISP
  blocked.
- **ByeDPI** and **Zapret** — bypassing blocks without a server of your own. Zapret works right in the router's
  kernel (NFQUEUE) and can do what ByeDPI cannot.
- **Up to six additional exits**: different sites, devices and Wi-Fi networks through different servers.
- A watchdog with backup servers, encrypted DNS (DoH, DoT, DoQ, DoH3, ODoH), ad blocking, access schedules, home
  access over AmneziaWG, traffic by device.

## About this model

- **ARM64 architecture** with memory to spare — several protocols can run at once.
- **About 20 MB of flash.** Protocols are the heaviest part, and they can move to a **USB stick**: only the protocol
  programs or the whole system. If the stick is pulled out, the router keeps working direct, without the VPN.
- **Mi Home and the smart home.** Xiaomi's servers in China are best sent past the VPN — in the panel that is the geo
  category `cn` with the action «bypass».

## How to install

1. Download the setup wizard from the [Releases](https://github.com/Axel173/enodia/releases) page.
2. Run it on a computer on the router's network — it opens SSH on the BE7000 and installs the web panel.
3. In the panel at `http://192.168.31.1:8088` add a server and choose what goes through the VPN.
